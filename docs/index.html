      About

      How this works

      Counting stats are converted to per-90 rates and pooled over each player's latest two qualifying seasons (at least 450 minutes per season). Players are compared only inside their position group: goalkeeper, centre-back, full-back, DM/CM, AM/winger or striker. Each stat becomes a percentile within its group for the radar. Similarity is computed on standardised stats: the average gap in standard deviations across the stats two players share is mapped to a 0 to 1 score, 1 / (1 + gap). Roles come from a simple KMeans clustering inside each group.

      The similarity percentage is a relative ranking, not "percent identical". Even the best matches are often around 50 to 70 percent.

      Find a signing ranks players from the filtered pool by average percentile, by average percentile per million euros (most undervalued), or by age. A Value pick is a player in the top quartile of average percentile for the group whose market value is in the bottom half of the group.

      Limitations

      • Only the Premier League, La Liga and Bundesliga are covered. Players from other leagues are not included.
      • FBref removed its advanced tables for 2025/26, so tackles, clearances, blocks, aerial duels and similar stats come from 2024/25 only. About 20 percent of defenders and midfielders (mostly new signings) lack them and are compared on the stats they have. Stats are per 90 and can be noisy over a small number of minutes.
      • Centre-back matches still lean on cards, fouls and height, which say little about playing style. A re-weighting is planned.
      • Goalkeeper matches use saves, shots faced, save percentage and goals conceded while on the pitch, which partly reflect the team.
      • Only the 20 closest matches per player are precomputed, so strict filters can hide most of them.
      • Market values, ages and contract dates are a snapshot from the data build. Some contract dates are out of date.
      • Similarity measures statistical style and level, not tactics, role instructions or league strength.
